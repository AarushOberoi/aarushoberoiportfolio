O u r S t o r y

In 2018, a few tech enthusiasts at Indraprastha World School got together with their head teacher of computer science and decided to build something real. Not a class project. Not a committee. A collective for people who genuinely loved technology. They called it OPCODE.

When the pandemic hit, the momentum held. Members used that time to go deeper into what they loved. When schools reopened, OPCODE brought it back through free in-school workshops covering graphic design, video editing, 3D animation, photography, filmmaking, robotics and more. The club grew through recruitments, through every event we ran and every competition we entered. Seven years on, we are 40 plus members strong and still building.
